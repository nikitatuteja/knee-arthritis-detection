How ArthroScan Works

A sophisticated 3-step process for clinical-grade analysis.

1

Image Preprocessing

Your X-ray is normalized and resized to optimize features for our neural network architecture.

2

Feature Extraction

The AI identifies key markers like joint space narrowing, osteophytes (bone spurs), and subchondral sclerosis.

3

KL-Grade Prediction

Final classification is generated based on the Kellgren-Lawrence scale with a detailed confidence breakdown.

Understanding Severity Levels

We classify based on the Kellgren-Lawrence grading system.

0

Normal

Healthy joint space with no signs of arthritis or bone spurs.

1

Doubtful

Possible joint space narrowing and subtle bone spur formation.

2

Mild

Definite bone spurs with possible joint space narrowing.

3

Moderate

Multiple moderate bone spurs and definite joint space narrowing.

4

Severe

Large bone spurs, marked narrowing, and bone deformity.

About the Project

ArthroScan AI was developed as a tool for automated diagnosis of knee osteoarthritis severity. By leveraging convolutional neural networks trained on thousands of validated X-ray images, we aim to provide a reliable secondary diagnostic tool for healthcare professionals and researchers.
